Learn contract bridge one short drill at a time. Bridge Trainer covers what new players need most: counting points, choosing an opening bid, planning the play as declarer and defending with partner. Every answer comes with the reasoning, so the idea sticks before your next rubber.

Nobody is waiting on you to bid, and there is no account to set up. Pick a room, work through a few hands, and come back tomorrow.

FOUR ROOMS, FREE FOR GOOD

The Card Room
Card rank, high-card points, tricks, trump, contracts and the four seats at the table, explained on swipeable coaching cards.

The Auction Room
A beginner Standard American framework. Read a full 13-card hand, count it, and decide between a pass, a one-level suit opening and 1NT.

The Declarer Room
Count your winners, draw trump, set up a long suit and take a finesse. Each deal asks which card you would play and shows the coach's play.

The Defense Room
Opening leads, leading from a sequence, third hand high, returning partner's suit and other habits good defenders share.

HOW A SESSION GOES
Coaching cards flip with a tap. Questions grade the moment you answer and keep the explanation on screen until you move on. Card-play deals let you tap the card you would play. A daily streak and a mixed review session bring back what you missed.
