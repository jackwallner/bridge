## Notes

- `project.yml` pins RevenueCat `from: 5.72.0`, so builds take the newest 5.x (5.102.3 on the runner today), not the 5.80.3 the review read. SM-3 depends on SDK fetch-policy semantics. A pinned exact version, or a re-read on each SDK bump, would keep the guard honest.
- SM-3 has one residual window, below the bar for a failure. If the app is in the background when `confirmMembership()` runs, RevenueCat counts a cache up to 25 hours old as fresh (`cacheDurationInSeconds(isAppBackgrounded:)`). A member who re-subscribed outside the app inside that window, and who leaves the app within seconds of a cold launch, could lose the reminder on a day-old real "not a member". It needs a purchase made outside the app plus a backgrounded launch.
- SM-3 confirms once per process. A launch that stays unconfirmed (offline, or a failed sync) is not retried until the next cold launch. That is conservative: the reminder stays.
- SM-4: ledger days saved before the upgrade have no scope, so during the first week after the update the old fake gain can still appear once if such a day was recorded as a free player and the player re-subscribes. The fix cannot infer the scope of old rows; the effect ends within 7 days.
- SM-1: launch fires both `onAppear` and the `.active` scene-phase hook, so a notDetermined toggle sends two `requestAuthorization` calls. iOS shows one alert. This is harmless as far as the code shows and was not verifiable headless.
- No new user-facing strings in the diff, so no `Localizable.xcstrings` work was needed. No widget or watch targets exist. No layout changed, so iPhone Duo layouts are unaffected.
- The fixes doc cites first-branch commit ids (6cac423, 8d0e2da, 65bf6ca, 819b075, d69684f, 573e9cb, 62173a2, fb0d98d). On this branch the same changes are 2d3e310, 7ce668f, 9a5569a, 2d038a6, b14cb14, e5296c0, 1b21770 and fdd2599.
