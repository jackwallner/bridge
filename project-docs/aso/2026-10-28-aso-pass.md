## Decisions

- Kept the U.S. title `Bridge Trainer: Learn & Bid`. It remains #1 for `bridge trainer` and #7 for `learn bridge` in the live SERP snapshot.
- Changed the U.S. subtitle to `5-Minute Drills for Beginners`, the August audit recommendation. The app is #57 for the autocomplete query `bridge for beginners`; `bridge drills` ranked #16 in the August audit. Current rank for `bridge drills` could not be refreshed.
- Repaired the Turkish subtitle `konuşma` (conversation) with the bridge-auction term `ihale`, and corrected the German subtitle’s `Brücke` (a physical bridge) to the card-game term `Bridge`.
- Cleaned the target keyword fields in their local languages. Most edits remove exact repeats of visible name or subtitle words, while keeping other existing terms whose current ranks could not be refreshed. The U.K. keyword field is unchanged because the search API block prevented confirming whether `points` could replace `score`.
- Refreshed the first two description paragraphs and promotional text for tr, nl-NL, th, de-DE, da, fr-FR, el, and en-GB. The remaining descriptions, four-room free tier, Bridge+ features, trial terms, partnership convention note, and privacy links are unchanged.
- The ru, no, ko, sv, es-ES, and es-MX descriptions contain no English-only claim, so the parallel in-app localizations did not require a description edit.
