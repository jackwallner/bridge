How it's built.

The Pitch

Most people learn bridge at a weekly game or a beginner class, then have no way to practice until the next one. The lesson fades, the auction gets scary, and the same mistakes come back.

Bridge Trainer takes the parts beginners need most and turns them into short drills you can run standing in line: count the points, choose a bid, plan the play, pick a lead. No opponents, and an explanation on every answer.

Bridge+

The four beginner rooms stay free. Bridge+ is for players who want more reps and more depth: monthly or yearly (both with a 1-week free trial), or a one-time purchase for lifetime access. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Extra practice sets. A second set of hands and questions in every beginner room, using the same drills you already know.

The Master Tables. Conventions, advanced declarer play and defense, and duplicate strategy, added to through the year.

What Mattered

Short sessions. A session is short enough to finish while the kettle boils. Nothing in the app asks you to sit down for an hour.

Reasoning over scoring. Every answer explains the bridge logic, because a beginner who knows why they were wrong improves faster than one who just sees a score.

Real 13-card hands. Drills use full deals rather than abstract puzzles, so the judgment transfers to the table.

What's free. All four beginner rooms are complete without paying. Bridge+ adds volume and advanced material; the basics are never locked.

Questions

Is there an app to practice bridge bidding?

Yes. Bridge Trainer is a free iPhone and iPad app with an Auction Room where you read a 13-card hand and choose the opening bid in a beginner Standard American framework. Every answer explains the reasoning.

What is the best app to learn contract bridge as a beginner?

Bridge Trainer is built for beginners who are learning or relearning contract bridge. It covers card basics, bidding, declarer play and defense in five-minute drills, with a coaching card behind every call.

Is Bridge Trainer free?

Yes, the download is free and the four beginner practice rooms, coaching cards and streaks are included. An optional Bridge+ upgrade adds extra practice sets and the Master Tables, and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no sign-up, and every drill works offline. Your progress stays on your device.

How do I learn bridge bidding?

Start by counting high-card points, then practice choosing an opening bid from real 13-card hands. The Auction Room in Bridge Trainer drills pass, suit openings and 1NT, and explains why each bid is right.

Can I practice declarer play and defense on my phone?

Yes. The Declarer Room covers counting winners, drawing trump, establishing long suits and finessing, and the Defense Room covers opening leads, sequences, third-hand play and suit returns.

Does it play against opponents?

No. Bridge Trainer is practice, not a table. There are no opponents or bots, so you can work through hands at your own pace between games.

Bridge Trainer teaches a beginner Standard American framework. Partnership agreements and conventions vary, so confirm what you play with your partner.
