## Bridge Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Bridge Trainer works without an account. Practice progress, review
history, streaks, reminders, and preferences stay on your device.

## Purchases

Apple processes Bridge+ purchases. RevenueCat verifies subscription
status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and
entitlement information, and limited technical app or device context.
To learn which screens lead to a membership, the app also sends
RevenueCat counts of where and how often the Bridge+ offer was shown,
the dates of the first offer and of a purchase, how many times the app
was opened before the first offer, and which screen a purchase started
from. It does not receive your practice content or name. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Feedback

On iPhone and iPad, Send Feedback and the one question asked when a
membership is ending send what you write straight to the developer.
Supabase stores it. Each message also carries the app version, iOS
version, device model, language and region setting, and whether you are
a Bridge+ member. No name, email or account is attached. Messages are
read to fix problems and improve the app, and are kept only as long as
that takes. Because nothing ties a message to you, email us what you
sent and roughly when if you want it removed.

## Ad attribution

If you install Bridge Trainer from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple's
AdServices framework tells RevenueCat which campaign and keyword led to
the install. This uses no advertising identifier and does not track you
across other apps. We use it only to measure whether our ads pay for
themselves.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location,
health data, or advertising identifier. We do not use advertising or
behavioral analytics SDKs.

## Permissions, retention, and deletion

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally and can be disabled in the
app or iOS Settings. Local data remains until you delete it or remove
the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records as needed to
process and restore purchases. Email support messages contain whatever
you choose to send and are used to answer your request.

## Children and changes

Bridge Trainer is not directed to children under 13 and does not
knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this
policy when the app or its services change.
