## Frequently asked questions

**Why can bidding advice differ from my club?**
Bridge
partnerships use different agreements. The app presents a beginner
Standard American framework and explains the assumption behind each
answer.

**How do I restore Bridge+?**
Open Settings, choose
Bridge+, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for
the original purchase.

**How do I cancel a subscription?**
Manage subscriptions
in your Apple Account settings. Apple handles billing and cancellation.
