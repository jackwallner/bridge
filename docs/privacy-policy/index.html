Bridge Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Bridge Trainer works without an account. Practice progress, review history, streaks, reminders, and preferences stay on your device.

Purchases

Apple processes Bridge+ purchases. RevenueCat verifies subscription status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical app or device context. It does not receive your practice content or name. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, health data, or advertising identifier. We do not use advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

Permissions, retention, and deletion

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally and can be disabled in the app or iOS Settings. Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records as needed to process and restore purchases. Email support messages contain whatever you choose to send and are used to answer your request.

Children and changes

Bridge Trainer is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
