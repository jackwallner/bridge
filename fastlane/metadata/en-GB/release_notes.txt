A smarter rhythm for the table.

Bridge Minute is a new daily challenge. Every member gets the same five questions on the same day: two opening calls, one declarer decision, and two defensive judgments. Share your score, and keep a forgiving five-day weekly rhythm with a 30-day archive to catch up from.

Game Night Prep lets you set your usual bridge night. A reminder arrives beforehand and opens straight into a five-minute session built from your due mistakes, your weakest room, and material you have not seen yet.

Bridge Trainer now runs natively on iPad, in portrait or landscape.

Bridge Minute and Game Night Prep are part of Bridge+.
